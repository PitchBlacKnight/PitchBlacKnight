I.Summary

A designer obsessed with visual craft, 23 years making complex work beautiful: typography, spacing, color, motion, and the interaction details most teams never get to. Deep history in regulated healthcare and enterprise products, as a core contributor to GE Healthcare's Edison Design System (2020 Red Dot for Interface Design) and the builder of Blue Cross Blue Shield's FIBER system. Five design systems built, plus contributions inside mature systems at GE and Verizon, carried today as fluency rather than specialty: comfortable in any system, focused on making the work inside it exceptional. Accessibility built in by default. Alongside that, most of my discretionary time now goes to generative AI and what it changes about how creative gets made, with the same rigor I bring to a type scale. Principal Designer at PBK Studio. Complex work, made beautiful, built to scale.

II.Generative AI Practice
Hands-on, and honest about the mapproduction work, not demos

Adobe Firefly and Photoshop generative fill and expand in production for image extension, reframing across placements, and cleanup, chosen partly because it is the model with a commercial indemnification path. ChatGPT and Claude daily for concepting, naming, copy iteration, and image work. Figma Make for taking a direction to a clickable prototype in an afternoon. Still in discovery on Midjourney, Runway, and Veo, running the same brief through each and scoring on brand fidelity, control across a set, edit-ability, cost per usable asset, and rights posture. I would rather show a team an honest comparison than overstate a tool I have used twice.

Method, written downthe systems habit, applied

Authored a generative AI creative standard for regulated categories: layered prompt architecture, house style blocks, a negative-prompt library written against implied-claim and synthetic-clinician risk, seed and reference control for set consistency, naming and versioning, model rights posture, a review packet for legal, and metrics for whether any of it is working. Built because prompt lists go stale in a month and the layer underneath them does not.
