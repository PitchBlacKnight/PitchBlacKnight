You are looking for someone fluent in AI who can improve your AI outcomes through experimentation. Plenty of designers can make a striking generative image. Far fewer can make a hundred of them stay on brand, and fewer still can do that inside a regulated healthcare category where every asset meets a reviewer before it meets a member. I have spent most of twenty years in that category, at GE Healthcare, Blue Cross Blue Shield, AbbVie, and Catamaran, and the last stretch running my creative process through GenAI. That intersection is the whole pitch.

I.My read on the problem

The volume is the constraint

A two-brand consumer portfolio running continuous paid social, email, display, and landing work needs more finished variants than a lean in-house team can hand-build. That gap is exactly what generative production closes.

Efficiency is the actual brief

The public story this year is a tighter media and cost posture. So the win is not novelty. The win is more usable, on-brand assets per hour and per dollar, without the brand loosening at the edges.

Review is where AI work dies

In healthcare, generative output runs into implied clinical claims, synthetic people who read as patients or clinicians, and model licensing that may or may not carry indemnification. Most teams discover this late.

You already said the thing

Teladoc's own AI position is about moving from experimentation to execution with governance and human oversight. Creative deserves the same treatment, and almost never gets it.

So the offer is this. Bring me in to experiment loudly and then do the unglamorous half: turn what works into a documented, repeatable house workflow your team can run without me, with the rights and review questions answered up front instead of discovered in legal. Experimentation that leaves something behind.
