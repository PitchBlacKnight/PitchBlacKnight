What survived, what changed, and why

HIGH DISCOVERABILITY Hero headline is the status

"1 task is waiting" - the watcher's only question, answered before any interaction. White kept for large text only; small text on orange goes dark for AA contrast.

HIGH Stat tiles - B's contribution

Done / Waiting / Next-at-a-glance. Glanceable state for the owner checking in from the airport; the amber tile is the only urgency signal, and it's quiet.

CONVERTED The deck became one card

Stacked decks are a browsing pattern - right for choosing a pet to adopt, wrong for executing care. Kept the cutout photography and tinted card; gave it a time, a task, the instruction that matters, and one CTA. Beauty now carries data.

HIGH Dashed tick → solid green

Dashed circle reads "awaiting you"; green check with attribution ("Sam · 7:12 am") is the trust record. Same one-tap optimistic completion + undo as the prototype.

HIGH Photo chips

Faces scan faster than names. Cutout-friendly studio photos mean these stay crisp at 26px and at full-card size from the same asset.
