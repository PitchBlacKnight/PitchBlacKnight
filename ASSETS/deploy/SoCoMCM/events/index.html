How Events Work

🏠
House Tours & Social Events

Members & Public

Home tours, cocktail receptions, and annual parties open the region's finest MCM residences to members and the public. Some events are members-only with limited capacity — join the mailing list to stay informed.

🎄
Member Gatherings

For the SoCoMCM Community

Annual celebrations, holiday pick-ups, and informal gatherings bring the membership together at member homes and curated venues. Members receive first notice and priority access to ticketed events.

🎙
Lectures & Education

Free & Open to the Public

Our lecture series covers historic landmark designation, architectural history, renovation tactics, and landscape planning — presented by leading experts. Free admission. The public is always welcome.
