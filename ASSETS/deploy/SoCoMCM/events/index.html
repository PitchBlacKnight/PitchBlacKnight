How Events Work

🏠
House Tours

Ticketed Access to Private Homes

Our house tours open privately owned MCM residences to the public. Tickets are required and space is limited. Advance registration is strongly recommended. Docents available at select properties.

📋
Research Meetings

Monthly, Open to All Members

Monthly Zoom sessions for active researchers. Share findings, take on new properties, and coordinate outreach. Zoom link sent to the mailing list. New members welcome — no prior experience required.

📷
Photography Walks

Free, All Skill Levels Welcome

Guided neighborhood walks to photograph and document MCM exteriors. Bring any camera. No registration required unless noted. Meet at the listed location. Good shoes recommended.
