#Philosophy

I don't separate
craft from conscience.
Never have.

01
"Clarity is a form of kindness. Design that confuses doesn't just frustrate — it excludes. Every choice I make asks: who does this leave behind?"
On accessibility, plain language, and making the obvious thing obvious for everyone.
02
"If your product requires confusion to convert, you've engineered extraction — not experience. I don't build those. I've turned down the work before and I'll do it again."
On dark patterns, predatory UX, and the designs I quietly walk away from.
03
"Structure is freedom. A great design system doesn't constrain creativity — it compounds it. The constraint is the invitation."
On design systems, token architecture, and why rules at scale are a form of generosity.
04
"The projects you decline define you as much as the ones you take. I'm selective by design — because the work shapes the designer."
On choosing mission-driven work, healthcare, education, and things built to serve rather than extract.
