06 — Daily Wisdom

Most of what I know

I learned by doing it wrong first.

The rest came from watching

what other people needed.

Good work doesn't announce itself.

It just makes the thing easier to use,

easier to hear,

easier to feel.

I'm still figuring things out.

I think that might be the whole point.
